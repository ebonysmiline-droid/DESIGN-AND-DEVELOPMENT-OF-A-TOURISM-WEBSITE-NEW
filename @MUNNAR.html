Popular Tourist Attractions

1. Eravikulam National Park

Eravikulam National Park is famous for its natural beauty, mountain landscapes and Nilgiri Tahr. Visitors can enjoy wildlife and nature sightseeing.

2. Mattupetty Dam

Mattupetty Dam is a beautiful destination surrounded by green hills. Visitors can enjoy boating and peaceful lake views.

3. Tea Museum

The Tea Museum explains the history of tea production in Munnar. Visitors can learn about tea processing and different varieties of tea.

4. Top Station

Top Station is famous for its beautiful valley views, misty mountains and scenic landscapes. It is a popular photography destination.

Why Visit Munnar?

Munnar is one of the most beautiful hill stations in Kerala. It offers peaceful surroundings, green tea plantations, beautiful waterfalls, wildlife and unforgettable mountain views. It is an ideal destination for families, friends and nature lovers.
