Welcome to Coorg, Karnataka

Explore the beautiful hill station of Coorg, famous for its coffee plantations, green mountains, waterfalls, rivers and peaceful natural scenery.

Popular Tourist Attractions in Coorg

1. Abbey Falls

Abbey Falls is a famous waterfall located near Madikeri. It is surrounded by coffee plantations and green forests. Visitors can enjoy the beautiful waterfall view.

2. Raja's Seat

Raja's Seat is a popular viewpoint in Madikeri. It is famous for its beautiful gardens, green valleys and spectacular sunrise and sunset views.

3. Talakaveri

Talakaveri is located in the Brahmagiri Hills of Coorg. It is traditionally regarded as the origin of the sacred River Kaveri and is an important pilgrimage destination.

4. Dubare Elephant Camp

Dubare Elephant Camp is situated near the River Kaveri. Visitors can learn about elephants, enjoy the forest surroundings and experience nature.

Why Visit Coorg?

Coorg is one of the most beautiful tourist destinations in Karnataka. It is famous for its coffee plantations, misty mountains, waterfalls, wildlife, temples and peaceful landscapes. It is an ideal destination for families, friends and nature lovers.
