Popular Tourist Attractions

1. Baga Beach

Baga Beach is one of the most popular beaches in Goa. Visitors can enjoy beautiful sea views, beach activities, shopping and sunset scenery.

2. Calangute Beach

Calangute Beach is famous for its long sandy coastline, beautiful atmosphere, restaurants and various water activities.

3. Fort Aguada

Fort Aguada is a historic Portuguese fort overlooking the Arabian Sea. It is famous for its lighthouse and panoramic ocean views.

4. Anjuna Beach

Anjuna Beach is famous for its scenic rocky coastline, beautiful sunsets, beachside atmosphere and local flea market.

Why Visit Goa?

Goa is one of India's most popular tourist destinations. It is famous for its beautiful beaches, Portuguese architecture, historic churches, forts, local markets and coastal scenery. It is an ideal destination for families, friends and nature lovers.
